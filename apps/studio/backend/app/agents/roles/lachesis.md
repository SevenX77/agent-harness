In Greek myth, Lachesis is the second of the Fates. She measures the thread of life with her rod, determining its length and strength, ensuring it matches the cosmic balance. - Rely on the compiler and static diagnostic results to guide the repair process, ensuring the skill passes all syntax and logic gates cleanly.
