## Act Breakdown

### Act 1: The Great Typhoon - Rebirth and Preparation (Chapters 1-10+)
**Hard Axis State:** Pre-Apocalypse -> **Typhoon & Initial Flood**. The world transitions from normalcy to the first stage of global catastrophe. Infrastructure (power, water, communication) begins to fail.
**Soft Axis State:** **Zero to Hero Security**. The protagonist starts with nothing but重生 knowledge and a small amount of cash. She rapidly acquires a spatial inventory, vast material wealth, and a fortified base. Her revenge begins by cutting off and verbally humiliating her past-life enemies.

**Sub-Acts:**
- **A段 (Chapters 1-3): The Hoarding Frenzy (疯狂囤货).** 
  - **Summary:** Waking up three days before the apocalypse, Jiang Ning reclaims her spatial pendant. She immediately liquidates her assets and begins a meticulous, large-scale hoarding operation. She purchases everything from food, medicine, and fuel to weapons, extreme weather gear, and seeds. The core satisfaction (爽点) is the detailed, almost obsessive, list-checking and acquisition of hundreds of billions in supplies, creating an overwhelming sense of security.
  - **Key Events:** Rebirth and realization (Ch 1), Acquiring the space (Ch 1), Massive shopping sprees across various wholesale markets (Ch 2-3), Fortifying her apartment with heavy-duty doors and glass (Ch 1).

- **B段 (Chapters 4-7): The Storm Arrives & First Conflicts (台风降临 & 初步冲突).**
  - **Summary:** The super typhoon makes landfall, earlier and more violently than predicted. Power, water, and communication networks start to fail. Jiang Ning confronts her past-life enemies, Yang Weicong and Su Mengyao, decisively crushing their attempts to leech off her by destroying the (now useless) pendant in front of them. As desperation sets in, she violently repels Yang Weicong's friends who come demanding food, establishing her ruthless attitude towards freeloaders.
  - **Key Events:** The typhoon hits (Ch 4), First direct confrontation with Yang Weicong and Su Mengyao (Ch 5), A Dragon Roll appears, signaling a more severe disaster than her past life (Ch 6), Jiang Ning violently threatens scavengers with a knife, solidifying her fierce reputation (Ch 7).

- **C段 (Chapters 8-10): Establishing a Safe Zone & Early Alliances (建立安全区 & 早期联盟).**
  - **Summary:** The floodwaters rise, trapping residents. Social order begins to break down. Jiang Ning solidifies her immediate surroundings by allying with her mysterious and capable new neighbor (Huo Yishen) to secure their floor. She also establishes contact with a trustworthy friend from her past life (Zheng Weili), offering her a safe haven. The focus shifts from pure hoarding to active defense, resource management (cooking and preserving food), and strategic social alignment.
  - **Key Events:** Teaming up with neighbor Huo Yishen to install a security door on their floor's stairwell (Ch 8), Water levels rise, trapping everyone (Ch 9), Rescuing her friend Zheng Weili and her partner, fighting off other desperate survivors in the process (Ch 10).

**Transition Hook:** The typhoon and initial flooding are just the beginning. The water continues to rise, hinting that the disaster will evolve into a full-blown flood that submerges the city. The alliances are new and fragile, and the threat from starving neighbors is escalating, setting the stage for a larger-scale social conflict within the apartment complex. The next Act will likely focus on survival during the prolonged flood.
