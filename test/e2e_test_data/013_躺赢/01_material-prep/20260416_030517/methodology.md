# Methodology Analysis

## Core Engine (核心引擎)
The story is driven by a dual-core engine of **Apocalyptic Survival and Revenge (末世生存与复仇)**.

1.  **Apocalyptic Survival:** The primary driver is the protagonist's struggle to survive a sequence of escalating, world-ending natural disasters (台风, 洪水, 极寒, 极热, etc.). The core appeal (爽点) lies in the meticulous preparation (囤货 - hoarding), leveraging future knowledge to create a position of overwhelming resource advantage, and calmly weathering cataclysms that plunge the rest of the world into chaos. This is a power fantasy of foresight and preparedness.

2.  **Revenge (虐渣):** A powerful secondary driver is the protagonist's desire for vengeance against those who wronged her in her past life, specifically her ex-boyfriend (杨伟聪) and his partner (苏梦瑶). The satisfaction comes from witnessing their downfall and suffering, directly contrasting with her own well-prepared, comfortable existence. Each interaction is an opportunity for "face-slapping" and demonstrating her newfound power and independence.

## Hard Axis (硬轴)
The Hard Axis is the **Sequence of Natural Disasters (天灾序列)**. This provides the story's primary structure and pacing. Each new disaster introduces a fundamental shift in the environment, survival challenges, and resource requirements. The progression is linear and escalating:

-   **Typhoon (台风)** -> **Flood (洪水)** -> **Extreme Cold (极寒)** -> **Extreme Heat (极热)** -> **Earthquake (地震)**, etc.

An arc is defined by the duration of one major disaster cycle.

## Soft Axis (软轴)
The Soft Axis is the protagonist's **Security Level & Revenge Progression (安全感水平 & 复仇进度)**.

-   **Security Level:** This is a measure of the protagonist's power, quantified by the volume and quality of her hoarded supplies (物资), the defensibility of her shelter (安全屋), the reliability of her allies (盟友), and her personal combat capabilities. The core loop involves converting monetary wealth into tangible survival security before the old world's economy collapses.

-   **Revenge Progression:** This tracks the state of her antagonists. It progresses from simply avoiding them, to verbally humiliating them, to watching them descend into desperation, and ultimately to ensuring they face the full, brutal consequences of the apocalypse without any of the advantages they stole from her in the previous timeline.

## Act Boundary Rule (大阶段拆分依据)
A major Act concludes when **one dominant disaster phase ends and the transition to the next, fundamentally different disaster begins.** This forces a complete strategic overhaul. For example, the end of the "Flood" Act and the beginning of the "Extreme Cold" Act represents a boundary. This is often accompanied by the resolution of a major social conflict or the definitive defeat of a local antagonist group, allowing the protagonist to consolidate her power before the next challenge.

## Sub-Act Logic (阶段内逻辑)
Each Act follows a three-part rhythm tailored to the survival-hoarding genre:

-   **A段: Preparation & Foreshadowing (准备与铺垫):** The protagonist leverages her foreknowledge to prepare for the *upcoming* disaster. This is the "hoarding" phase (囤货爽点), where satisfaction comes from the detailed process of acquiring resources. This part also foreshadows the specific challenges of the next disaster and sets up the initial social landscape.

-   **B段: Disaster & Conflict (天灾与冲突):** The disaster arrives, transforming the environment. The protagonist faces both external threats (the disaster itself) and internal threats (desperate survivors, antagonists). The appeal (爽点) comes from her using her preparations to live in relative comfort and safety, while simultaneously using her strength to confront and defeat those who threaten her (生存与虐渣爽点).

-   **C段: Resolution & Power Display (收尾与立威):** The peak of the crisis passes. The protagonist resolves the immediate conflicts, consolidates her position, and often engages in a "power display" (e.g., eating lavishly while others starve), reinforcing her superior status. This phase demonstrates the success of her preparation and sets the hook for the next Act by revealing the nature of the *next* looming disaster.
